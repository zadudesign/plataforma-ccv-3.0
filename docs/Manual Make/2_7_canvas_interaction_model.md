# 2.7. Canvas Interaction Model

The Scenario Builder canvas aligns with modern design standards and supports mouse, trackpad, and touchscreen navigation.

---

## 2.7.1. Navigation & Input Devices

### Input Device Modes
- **Auto-detect**: Automatically detects and switches controls based on the connected input device.
- **Mouse**: Designed for standard or Apple Magic Mouse.
- **Trackpad**: Optimized for trackpads and hybrid setups (two-finger slide to zoom or pan).
- **Touchscreen**: Supports touch panning and pinch-to-zoom gestures.

---

## 2.7.2. Panning Interaction Models

You can customize canvas navigation preferences under **Canvas Options > Input device settings**.

### 1. Right-Click Interaction Model (Default)
- **Pan canvas**: Right-click and drag (RMB drag), `Space + Left-click drag`, or Middle mouse button drag (MMB). Trackpad: 2-finger swipe or secondary click drag. Touchscreen: Touch and drag.
- **Zoom**: Scroll mouse wheel or pinch trackpad/screen.
- **Select single element**: Left-click (LMB) or tap.
- **Context menu**: Right-click (RMB) or secondary click.
- **Multi-select (one-by-one)**: `Ctrl/⌘ + Left-click` or `Shift + Left-click`.
- **Multi-select (marquee box)**: Left-click and drag (LMB drag) on empty canvas or `Shift/Ctrl + Left-click drag`.
- **Move element**: Left-click and drag on element.

### 2. Left-Click Interaction Model (Toggled)
- **Pan canvas**: Left-click and drag (LMB drag) or Middle mouse button drag (MMB). Trackpad: One-finger press and drag.
- **Multi-select (marquee box)**: Right-click and drag (RMB drag) or `Ctrl/⌘ + Left-click drag`.

### Settings Persistence
- Navigation preferences are saved to your account session.
- Settings reset to default if browser site data is cleared, incognito mode is opened, or a new device/browser is used.
